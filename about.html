About The Blowtorch

A public-facing home for the club, focused on amateur radio, fellowship, technical learning, and service.

Who we are

The Blowtorch of Parma Amateur Radio Club brings radio amateurs together to share knowledge, support the hobby, encourage new operators, and enjoy amateur radio as a community.

This simplified website is designed to keep the club's public information easy to find without requiring visitors or members to create accounts.

What you'll find here

Club information, downloadable documents and reference material, solar and propagation conditions, and ways to contact the club.

Additional public pages can be added later without rebuilding an entire membership system.
